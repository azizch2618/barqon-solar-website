Our Journey

From Vision to Reality

Founded in Multan, BARQON emerged from a simple observation: the Pakistani solar market lacked engineering precision and technical transparency. Clients were being sold systems based on guesswork rather than data.

Engr M Hassan established BARQON to bridge this gap. By combining advanced electrical engineering with a passion for renewable energy, we've helped hundreds of families and businesses in Multan and across Pakistan slash their bills and secure their energy future.
